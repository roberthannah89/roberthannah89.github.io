Faulhorn 2680 m

Schynige Platte → Faulhorn → Bachalpsee → First — SAC T2 · ~600 m gain · ~16 km point-to-point · ~6 h

"One of the most spectacular and most-walked panoramaweg traverses in Switzerland — a continuous front-row view of the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau across the Lütschental from a high alpine balcony."
— Berner Oberland tourism (paraphrased)

The Schynige Platte → First traverse is the classic Berner Oberland panorama hike. It threads a high ridge between two mountain railways: the historic Schynige Platte Bahn (rack railway from Wilderswil, in service since 1893) and the Firstbahn gondola down to Grindelwald. Optional summit detour to Faulhorn (2680 m, +130 m) and the iconic photo stop at Bachalpsee.

Critical: the Schynige Platte Bahn is summer-only — typically late May to late October. No winter access. Start early: limited service, slow rack railway up, and afternoon thunderstorm risk on the fully exposed ridge.

Routes (the classic panoramaweg, plus shorter variants)

1. Schynige Platte → Faulhorn → Bachalpsee → First (planned route) T2

  • Schynige Platte (2068 m) → Loucherhorn / Männdlenen (~2.5 h, gentle ridge with Eiger / Mönch / Jungfrau view the entire way). Optional refreshments at the historic Berghaus Männdlenen / Weberhütte (2344 m).
  • Männdlenen → Faulhorn (~1.5 h, ~250 m additional climb to the summit). The summit detour is short, well-marked, and rewarded with one of the great 360° panoramas of the Bernese Oberland — Eiger / Mönch / Jungfrau south, Brienzersee + Thunersee + Jura north.
  • Faulhorn → Bachalpsee (~1 h, ~400 m descent). The classic photo stop: Bachalpsee (2265 m) reflecting the Schreckhorn / Wetterhorn / Finsteraarhorn ice giants on a calm morning.
  • Bachalpsee → First (~1 h, mostly level / gently descending on a wide path). Finish at the Firstbahn top station and ride the gondola down to Grindelwald.
  • Total: ~16 km, ~600 m gain, ~500 m descent, ~6 h moving (SAC) including the Faulhorn detour.

2. Reverse direction: First → Bachalpsee → Faulhorn → Schynige Platte T2

  • Same trail in reverse. Most guides recommend Schynige Platte → First because the climb to Faulhorn happens early on fresh legs and you finish on a fast gondola, not the slow rack railway.
  • Reverse direction is sensible if afternoon Schynige Platte trains are full or if you're starting from Grindelwald and want the easier rail return.

3. Short variant: First → Bachalpsee → First (out-and-back) T1

  • 1 h each way on a wide, easy path. The most-walked half-day hike from First, suitable for families.
  • If weather looks marginal or time is tight, this is the way to still see Bachalpsee.
  • T1 — but at 2200 m+ — bring layers, water, and sun protection.

4. Faulhorn-only: First → Bachalpsee → Faulhorn → First T2

  • ~5 h round-trip from First. Skips the long Schynige Platte ridge but still gets the Faulhorn summit and Bachalpsee.
  • Useful if the Schynige Platte Bahn is closed (early/late season) or fully booked.

Getting There

By car

Drive to Wilderswil (large pay parking at the Schynige Platte Bahn valley station) or Grindelwald. Car-shuttle problem: the route ends at First (above Grindelwald), ~25 km by road from Wilderswil. The cleanest option is to leave the car at Wilderswil, do the traverse, then take the train back from Grindelwald → Interlaken Ost → Wilderswil at the end of the day.

By public transport (recommended)

  • Train: Zürich HB → Bern → Interlaken Ost (≈2 h, hourly InterCity).
  • Interlaken Ost → Wilderswil (5 min, BOB Berner Oberland-Bahn).
  • Wilderswil → Schynige Platte (~52 min on the historic rack railway, summer only).
  • Day end: First → Grindelwald gondola; Grindelwald → Interlaken Ost (~30 min) → Bern → Zürich HB (~2.5 h end-to-end from First).
  • Plan via sbb.ch — the SBB app handles the BOB and Schynige Platte Bahn legs end-to-end.
  • Tickets: a Berner Oberland Pass or GA Travelcard covers most of this; otherwise individual tickets at the station.

Day Plan (T2 traverse, single day)

Summer hike only. Best window: mid-July to late September — Schynige Platte Bahn typically runs late May to late October, but the ridge can hold snow patches until early July. Verify Schynige Platte Bahn opening dates before your trip — there is no winter access.

Trip Reports (hikr.org)

Key takeaways

  • The Eiger / Mönch / Jungfrau panorama is the whole point. Trip reports unanimously call this one of the best half-day-to-full-day panorama walks in the Alps — wall-to-wall views of the Berner Oberland giants for almost the entire ridge.
  • Direction matters. Schynige Platte → First is the standard direction: you climb to Faulhorn early on fresh legs and finish on a fast gondola, not the slow rack railway.
  • Bachalpsee at the right time of day. For the famous reflection photo, hit the lake in the morning before the afternoon breeze stirs the surface — favouring an early Schynige Platte start.
  • Crowds. July / August weekends are very busy; the Bachalpsee → First section in particular can feel like a parade. Weekday or shoulder-season starts are noticeably quieter.
  • The Faulhorn summit detour is short and worth it. ~30 min and ~130 m extra; Berghotel Faulhorn (1832, one of the oldest mountain hotels in Europe) is just below the summit and serves food in season.

Gear Checklist

Mandatory

  • Sturdy hiking boots or trail shoes (well-marked Wanderweg; T2 — but rocky in places)
  • 2–3 L water + electrolytes (top up at Männdlenen / Faulhorn / First when in season)
  • Food + emergency snack (huts and First restaurant available, but don't rely on them)
  • Sun: hat, sunglasses, SPF 30+ — exposure is total above 2000 m
  • Layers: base + insulating + windproof / rainproof shell (ridge wind can be sharp)
  • Hat + light gloves (Faulhorn summit can be near freezing even in summer)
  • Phone with offline SwissTopo map + GPX
  • Small first-aid kit
  • Cash / card for the Schynige Platte Bahn, Firstbahn, and huts

Recommended

  • Trekking poles (long descent from Faulhorn to Bachalpsee)
  • Camera or phone with charged battery — Bachalpsee is one of the Swiss photo stops
  • Power bank
  • Half-Fare Card / GA / Berner Oberland Pass to soften the rail + gondola cost

Safety & Hazards

  • Thunderstorms: the ridge is fully exposed for hours with no shelter between Schynige Platte and Männdlenen. Lightning is the dominant hazard — start early and turn around at the first sign of afternoon convection.
  • Mountain railway closure: the Schynige Platte Bahn is summer-only (typically late May to late October). Outside that window the entire traverse from the Schynige Platte side is inaccessible — verify opening dates before booking travel.
  • Last gondola: the Firstbahn last descent is typically ~17:00–17:30. Miss it and the only descent on foot is a long ~1500 m drop to Grindelwald (~2.5–3 h, T2).
  • Snow patches: shoulder-season snow can linger on the north-facing sections to Faulhorn into early July; be prepared to turn around if the trail is buried.
  • Sun + altitude: 5–6 h above 2000 m with no tree cover. Sunburn and dehydration are the most likely issues for fit hikers.
  • Cattle: the descent to Bachalpsee crosses pastures with mother cows — give space, no eye contact.
  • Phone reception: generally good along the Jungfrau-region ridges. Download maps offline anyway.
  • Emergency: Rega 1414 or 112. Berghotel Faulhorn and the First restaurant are useful safety nets.
  • This is a summer route. Winter access is not possible from the Schynige Platte side; the First side becomes a ski / snowshoe area with avalanche terrain.
