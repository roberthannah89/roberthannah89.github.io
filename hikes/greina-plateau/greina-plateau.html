Getting There

By car

Drive to Vrin via Sumvitg (Graubünden). From Chur, take the main road north via Ilanz toward Disentis; turn east into the Sumvitg valley. The road ends at the small village of Vrin (1453 m). Park near the village center or at the trailhead lots. Approximate drive from Zürich: ~2.5 hours.

By public transport

Train: Zürich HB → Disentis/Mustér (RhB Gotthard line, ~2.5 h). The famous "Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn" passes through Disentis. PostBus: Disentis/Mustér → Sumvitg → Vrin (line 570 or regional variant; ~45 min—1 h, seasonal/daily variations). Check PostBus timetable and SBB trip planner (combined ticket recommended). Return: Vrin → PostBus to Sumvitg/Disentis → Train back to Zürich or regional hub.

Lapse-rate temperature estimate

Mountain temperature drops about 6.5 °C per 1000 m.

  • Valley reference: Disentis / Sumvitg (~1630 m).
  • Summit: 2355 m → ~1755 m above the reference → subtract ~10.5 °C.
  • 18 °C at Disentis → ~13.5 °C at plateau (or cooler with wind chill; sustained winds can make -5 °C feel like -15 °C).
  • Pack for the cold end: hat, gloves, windproof, insulating layer.

Snow / season

July–September is the reliable window; August typically has the calmest weather and most reliable snow-free conditions. Early June can still have patches of hard snow above 2200 m. October is possible but increasingly risky (early frost, sudden weather swings). Avoid after fresh snow — the plateau becomes treacherous and navigation is difficult.
