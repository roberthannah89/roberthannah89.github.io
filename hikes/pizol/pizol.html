Pizol 2844 m

Wangs → Pizolhütte → five glacial lakes circuit → Pizol (2844 m) — SAC T2 · ~900 m gain · ~12 km · ~5–6 h

"One of Switzerland's most celebrated alpine walks — five glacial lakes gleaming like jewels below the Pizol massif. Each lake has its own character, from the dark mirror of the Schwarzsee to the turquoise shimmer of the Pizolsee at 2400 m."
— Glarus Alps hiking lore

The Pizol 5-Seen-Wanderung links five glacial lakes in a single circular walk above Pizolhütte (2227 m): Schwarzsee (2157 m), Baschalvasee (2232 m), Schottensee (2244 m), Pizolsee (2400 m) and Wildsee (2390 m). The circuit is rated T2 — wide mountain paths, no exposure — making it accessible to fit beginners. The optional push to the Pizol summit (2844 m) upgrades the day to T3 with a further 617 m of gain and panoramic views from the Rhine valley to the Bernina.

Routes (lake circuit T2; Pizol summit optional T3)

1. 5-Seen-Wanderung circuit — cable car from Wangs (recommended, T2) T2

  • Take the Pizolbahnen cable car from Wangs (506 m) or Bad Ragaz up to Pizolhütte (2227 m).
  • Head south-west from Pizolhütte, dropping slightly to Schwarzsee (2157 m) — the darkest, most brooding of the five lakes.
  • Climb west past Baschalvasee (2232 m) and Schottensee (2244 m), both reached on wide, well-marked paths.
  • Continue north over the Wildseelugge saddle to Pizolsee (2400 m) and then south-east to Wildsee (2390 m).
  • Return to Pizolhütte for lunch, then descend by cable car. Circuit ~10–11 km, ~650 m gain, ~4–5 h.
  • The five-lake sequence is clearly waymarked with the yellow hiking-path blazes.

2. + Pizol summit extension (T3, adds ~2 h and 617 m gain) T3

  • From Wildsee, follow the signed ridge path north-east to Pizol (2844 m). The final 200 m gain involves short scrambling sections on good holds — T3.
  • Summit panorama covers the Rhine Valley, Liechtenstein, the Säntis massif, the Glarus Alps and (on clear days) the Bernina.
  • Descend south back to Wildsee or directly to Pizolhütte via the marked descent ridge. Total with cable car: ~12 km, ~900 m gain, ~5–6 h.

3. Full ascent on foot from Wangs — no cable car (T2/T3, ~9–10 h) T3

  • Start in Wangs (506 m) and ascend through Pardiel, Gaffia, and Laufböden to Pizolhütte — ~1720 m gain over ~7 km.
  • Join the lake circuit at Pizolhütte. Continue to Pizol summit (optional).
  • Descent back to Wangs via the same route or via Furt. Total: ~16 km, ~2340 m gain — a long, demanding day.

Getting There

By car

Drive to Wangs (canton St. Gallen), signposted from the A13 motorway at junction Bad Ragaz. Paid parking at the Pizolbahnen base station. Alternatively park in Bad Ragaz — a gondola connects Bad Ragaz to Pardiel then Pizolhütte.

By public transport

  • Train: Zürich HB → Sargans (≈50 min, IC/IR) or → Bad Ragaz (≈55 min).
  • From Sargans: Postbus 190 to Wangs, Pizolbahnen (≈10 min).
  • From Bad Ragaz: local bus or short taxi to Wangs cable-car base, or use the Bad Ragaz gondola.
  • Plan via sbb.ch. The Pizolbahnen timetable and summer operating hours are at pizol.com.

Trip Reports (hikr.org)

Key takeaways

  • The cable car is the key logistical decision: Pizolbahnen runs from both Wangs and Bad Ragaz — the Bad Ragaz gondola has a longer ride and slightly more scenic approach, but Wangs is faster from Sargans by bus. Book or check operating hours in advance in shoulder season.
  • The five lakes are best done counter-clockwise (Schwarzsee → Baschalvasee → Schottensee → Pizolsee → Wildsee) so the steeper sections are ascents not descents, and you arrive at Wildsee with maximum energy for the optional Pizol summit push.
  • By late August the Pizolsee shrinks noticeably — earlier in summer (July) gives higher water levels and more dramatic reflections. The Schwarzsee is darkest in the morning when shadows pool in its cirque.
  • The summit of Pizol (T3) is a short but genuine scramble on good granite — poles should be stowed for the final section. Views extend to the Rheintal, Liechtenstein, Säntis, and on perfect days the Bernina massif to the south-east.

Safety & Hazards

  • Lightning: the ridge above Pizolhütte is fully exposed. Check MeteoSwiss before departure and descend to the hütte if convective cloud builds.
  • Early-season ice: the Wildseelugge saddle and paths near Pizolsee can be icy in early summer — microspikes recommended before mid-July.
  • Crowds: the 5-Seen-Wanderung is one of Switzerland's most popular hikes. Weekday starts avoid the worst congestion near the lakes.
  • T3 summit scramble: the Pizol summit section requires sure-footedness on good rock. Not recommended in wet conditions or for children without alpine experience.
  • Cable car schedule: missing the last gondola means a 1700 m descent on foot — always note the last departure time.
  • Phone reception: good at Pizolhütte and on the ridge; patchy in some lake cirques. Download maps offline.
  • Emergency: Rega 1414 · European emergency 112. Pizolhütte has a phone.
  • This is a summer route only. Winter and early-spring conditions involve avalanche terrain on the upper slopes.
