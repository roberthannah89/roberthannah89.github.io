Getting There

By car

Via Grindelwald: From Interlaken, drive north to Grindelwald (19 km, ~25 min on scenic mountain road). Park at the Grindelwald terminal parking (CHF 3/h) near Jungfraubahn station. Take the cog railway to Eigergletscher (see transit details above). Alternatively, park lower in Grindelwald and use the Alpiglen cable car for return descent to Grindelwald village.

By public transport

Train + Cog Railway: From Interlaken Ost, take the scenic railway to Grindelwald (~35 min). From Grindelwald station, follow signs to the Jungfraubahn terminal. Take the Jungfraubahn (cog railway) uphill toward Jungfraujoch; alight at Eigergletscher station (~40 min from Grindelwald). Swiss Travel Pass accepted on rail; Jungfraubahn requires additional ticket (CHF 50–70). Return via cable car from Alpiglen to Grindelwald (CHF 15) or hike down further.

Lapse-rate temperature estimate

Mountain temperature drops about 6.5 °C per 1000 m.

  • Valley reference: Interlaken (570 m) — reference station for Bernese Oberland.
  • Summit: 2320 m → ~1720 m above the reference → subtract ~10.3 °C.
  • 22 °C at Interlaken → ~11 °C at Eigergletscher
  • Pack for the cold end: hat, gloves, windproof, insulating layer.
