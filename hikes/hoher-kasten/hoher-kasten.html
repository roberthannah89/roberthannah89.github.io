Hoher Kasten 1795 m

Ridge walk from Brülisau — SAC T2 · ~1000 m gain · ~8 km · ~4–5 h · cable car option

"Hoher Kasten is the most prominent viewpoint of the Appenzell Alps — a 360° panorama encompassing the Rhine valley, the Bodensee, the Säntis massif, and the Vorarlberg mountains of Austria. The revolving restaurant at the summit turns the hard work optional."
— Appenzell tourism descriptions

Hoher Kasten (1795 m) is the flagship summit of Appenzell Ausserrhoden, reached either on foot from Brülisau (~1000 m gain, T2, ~3 h) or via the Luftseilbahn Brülisau–Hoher Kasten cable car (year-round). The classic objective is the ridge traverse towards Staubern (1635 m) and onward towards Saxer Lücke — a spectacular T2 walk with open views in all directions and almost no crowds once you leave the summit. Very popular on summer weekends; an early start or a shoulder-season visit rewards you with solitude.

Routes (T2 hiking routes — SAC mountain hiking grade)

1. Cable car + ridge traverse — recommended classic T2

  • Take the Luftseilbahn Brülisau–Hoher Kasten from Brülisau (year-round, ~10 min).
  • From the summit, follow the marked ridge trail south-west towards Staubern (1635 m, ~45 min one-way).
  • Continue ridge towards Saxer Lücke if time allows — the ridge opens up with views of both Appenzell valleys.
  • Return to Hoher Kasten by the same ridge; cable car down, or hike down to Brülisau.
  • Total ridge walking: ~4–5 km, ~2–3 h on the ridge itself.

2. Full ascent from Brülisau on foot — T2 T2

  • Start at Brülisau (~780 m); well-marked trail via Rohr alp ascending the south flank.
  • Ascent ~1000 m gain, ~3 h to summit.
  • Well-maintained hiking path, T2 throughout — steeper upper section but never exposed.
  • Descend via the same route or via the ridge to Staubern and down to Brülisau or Saxer Lücke.

3. Loop via Saxer Lücke — full day T2

  • Cable car up to Hoher Kasten; ridge traverse to Staubern then Saxer Lücke (~1652 m).
  • Descend from Saxer Lücke into the inner Appenzell valley or back towards Brülisau.
  • Total loop distance: ~12–14 km depending on variant; ~5–6 h.
  • Outstanding views throughout; stay alert for cattle on the open ridge.

Getting There

By car

Drive to Brülisau (Appenzell Ausserrhoden). Parking at the cable car valley station. From Zürich: ~1.5 h via A1 → St. Gallen → Appenzell road.

By public transport

  • Train: Zürich HB → St. Gallen (IC, ~70 min).
  • Appenzell Bahn: St. Gallen → Appenzell (~45 min).
  • Postbus: Appenzell → Brülisau (~20 min, line 222).
  • Total journey: ~2 h from Zürich. Plan via sbb.ch.
  • Cable car: Luftseilbahn Brülisau–Hoher Kasten runs year-round from Brülisau village.

Lapse-rate temperature estimate

Mountain temperature drops about 6.5 °C per 1000 m.

  • Valley reference: Appenzell village, ~780 m.
  • Summit: 1795 m → ~1015 m above the reference → subtract ~6 °C.
  • Example: Appenzell forecast 16–24 °C → Hoher Kasten summit ≈ 10 °C to 18 °C. Wind on the exposed ridge can make it feel significantly colder.
  • Pack for the cold end: hat, gloves, windproof, insulating layer.

Snow / season

Year-round accessible via cable car, but ridge walking is best from May to October. Snow and ice can make the ridge slippery in winter even at this modest elevation.

Trip Reports (hikr.org)

Key takeaways

  • The cable car is the most popular approach and justifies itself even for fit hikers — it's the ridge traverse that makes the day, not the ascent slog.
  • The ridge between Hoher Kasten and Staubern is well-marked, wide, and almost entirely T2 — a genuine family-friendly alpine walk with big views.
  • Arrive early on summer weekends: the summit restaurant and cable car both get very busy by 11:00. A Tuesday morning in August can feel like a private mountain.
  • On clear days the Bodensee is unmistakable to the north; the Säntis (2502 m) stands immediately to the south-east — a dramatic contrast in scale.

Safety & Hazards

  • Exposure: The ridge is broad and T2, but the summit plateau is exposed to wind — dress accordingly.
  • Thunderstorms: Afternoon storms develop rapidly in summer; be off the ridge by 13:00 if convection is forecast.
  • Cattle: Open ridge pastures have cattle — give space, especially mothers with calves.
  • Cable car: Check operating hours and last descent before setting out — see hoherkasten.ch for schedules.
  • Phone reception: Generally good on the summit; can be patchy on the lower ridge.
  • Emergency: Rega 1414 or 112.
  • Winter visit: the ridge can be icy even at 1700–1800 m; microspikes recommended outside summer season.
