Zindlenspitz 2097 m

Standard route from Innerthal — SAC T3 · ~1200 m gain · ~9.5 km one-way · ~6 h round-trip

"Zindlenspitz is one of the most characteristic mountains of Wägital. Viewed from the valley, it looks so uninviting that you hesitate to choose it as your hiking target. However, the steep limestone tooth is astonishingly easy to climb: T3, which does not even qualify the summit as an alpine hiking summit."
— SAC Route Portal

Also written Zindelspitz / Zindelnspitz; the SAC Route Portal uses Zindlenspitz. Elevation is 2097 m (the "2260" in the SAC URL is an internal ID, not the height).

Routes (from the SAC Route Portal)

1. From the south (Wägitalersee / Innerthal) — standard route T3

  • Source: SAC archive document Von Süden / Alpinführer Glarner Alpen (1), 11th ed. 2013, German.
  • Start: Innerthal / Wägitalersee (~915 m). Reachable by Postbus from Siebnen-Wangen.
  • Approach via Hohfläschen alps to the south-west flank.
  • Ascent gain: ~1200 m to the summit.
  • Variant exists at T5 on harder terrain near the summit ridge.

2. Brünnelistock + Rossälplispitz + Zindlenspitz — traverse T5−

  • Local nickname: Wägitaler Trilogie / Hohfläschentrilogie.
  • Three summits along the ridge; significant exposure and route-finding.
  • Not for beginners. Requires sure-footedness, scrambling, and good weather.

Getting There

By car

Drive to Innerthal (Schwyz). Parking near the Wägitalersee dam or in the village.

By public transport

  • Train: Zürich HB → Siebnen-Wangen (≈40–50 min, S-Bahn).
  • Postbus from Siebnen-Wangen / Schübelbach into the Wägital, terminating near Innerthal / Wägitalersee.
  • Plan via sbb.ch.

Lapse-rate temperature estimate

Mountain temperature drops about 6.5 °C per 1000 m.

  • Summit: 2097 m → ~1700 m above the reference → subtract ~11 °C.
  • Example: valley forecast 10–21 °C → summit ≈ −1 °C to +10 °C.
  • Pack for the cold end: hat, gloves, windproof, insulating layer.

Trip Reports (hikr.org)

Key takeaways

  • The first 4 km from Innerthal is flat shore-road walking along Wägitalersee — factor it into your start time and consider the Postbus to the Schiffstation if you want to skip it.
  • The standard T3 ascent goes via Aberliboden and Ziggenbach to Alp Zindlen, then zigzags through forest and crosses a rocky ridge before the final 130 m push from point 1604 to the summit.
  • The Trilogie traverse (Brünnelistock + Rossalpelispitz + Zindlenspitz) is firmly T5− with significant exposure — early start essential and not recommended in damp/wet conditions on the limestone.
  • Summit views span the inner Swiss Alps, the Wägitalersee, and the Glarner Oberseetal (turquoise lake visible to the SE).

Recent reports

Zindlenspitz: wie ein Flug über dem Wägital · summer · T3
  • Standard ascent from Innerthal: 4 km lakeside road first, then up the Ziggenbach side to Aberliboden.
  • Forest zigzags, rocky ridge crossing, then a relatively flat traverse at Alp Zindlen.
  • From p.1604 the final 130 m to the summit cross — dramatic Wägital "flight" panorama.
Trilogie: Brünnelistock + Rossalpelispitz + Zindlenspitz · summer · T5−
  • Three summits along the ridge in one outing — the classic Wägitaler Trilogie.
  • Significant exposure on the connecting ridges; sure-footedness and scrambling required.
  • Plan a long day with an early start; descent off Zindlenspitz is the standard T3 route.
Fluebrig–Zindlenspitz–Bockmattli — drei Voralpen-Gipfel · summer · T4–T5
  • Multi-summit day combining three of the most distinctive Voralpen peaks of the Wägital basin.
  • Different starting/ending points; logistics are easier with two cars or a long return walk.
  • Each peak is distinct in character — Zindlenspitz the tooth, Fluebrig the broad ridge, Bockmattli the pyramid.

Gear Checklist

Mandatory

  • Sturdy hiking boots (B-grade, ankle support)
  • 2–3 L water + electrolytes
  • Food + emergency snack
  • Sun: hat, sunglasses, SPF 30+
  • Layers: base + insulating + windproof / rainproof shell
  • Hat + gloves (summit can be near freezing)
  • Headlamp
  • Phone with offline SwissTopo map + GPX
  • Small first-aid kit
  • Cash / card for the Postbus

Recommended

  • Trekking poles
  • Power bank
  • Whistle

Trilogy traverse only

  • Helmet
  • Optional via-ferrata set / short rope
  • Significantly more time and an early start

Safety & Hazards

  • Exposure: T3 still means steep grass and rock; a slip can be serious.
  • Limestone: dries fast but slippery when wet — avoid in or just after rain.
  • Thunderstorms: ridge is fully exposed. Descend if weather builds.
  • Cattle: mother cows with calves can be defensive — give space.
  • Phone reception: patchy. Download maps offline.
  • Emergency: Rega 1414 or 112.
  • This is a summer route. Winter ascent involves avalanche terrain.
