Getting There

By car

From Chur head south via A13 towards Tirano (Italy). Follow signs to Poschiavo. Park at Ospizio San Gottardo (2064 m) on the Bernina Pass. (Note: Pass is typically open June–October; check road conditions in winter.)

By public transport

Bernina Railway (UNESCO heritage line): Take the RhB Bernina Express or regional train from Chur → Tirano, then return to Switzerland. Alight at Ospizio San Gottardo station on the Bernina line. Journey time ~2–2.5 hours from Chur.

Safety & Hazards

  • Glacier exposure & crevasses: Route crosses active glacial terrain; crevasse fall risk requires rope team & technical rescue capability. Late spring/early summer particularly hazardous.
  • Exposure & scrambling (T3+): Ridge sections involve hand-holds & exposed terrain; unroped scrambling with 300m+ cliff drops. Sure-footedness mandatory.
  • Weather deterioration: Rapid visibility loss; afternoon thunderstorm risk extreme at 2847m. Exposed ridge offers zero shelter.
  • Rockfall: Warming glacial slopes (mid-July–August) increase rockfall from above; loose rock on descent.
  • Route-finding: Cairns sparse; GPS/map essential in poor visibility. Glacier navigation complex without mountaineering experience.
  • Turn back if thunderstorms develop — lightning risk above treeline is extreme.
