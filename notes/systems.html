Systems

Systems make life easier and more efficient overall. Here are some of mine, refined over time. Don't reinvent — copying is easier than designing your own.

Travel Systems

Principles:

  • Always ready: These systems remain fully packed 100% of the time. When you travel, you simply throw them into your luggage and don't have to think about anything.
  • Completeness / over-packing: It is faster and simpler to over-pack than to manually decide what you need every time. Use the same system for a 2-day or 2-month trip.
  • Consistency: Pack everything in a consistent way so it's faster and easier to check whether everything is there when you depart and return, when replacing consumable items, etc.
  • Duplicates: The system contains duplicates of everything you use in your daily life. You buy duplicates so you don't need to pack. Keep replacements for consumable items like shampoo and conditioner ready to swap out.

Toiletries

This is a hanging toiletries bag with many compartments. You hang it on the towel rack of your accommodation. A laminated card is kept inside listing all contents and locations for easy packing and to avoid forgetting anything on the road.

  • Outside flap: Sunscreen, condoms.
  • Top flap: Shampoo, conditioner, body wash.
  • Bottom flap: Facial cleanser, moisturizer, retinol, vitamin C.
  • Vertical pockets: Manual razor, electric razor, toothbrushes.
  • Main section: Pommade, retainer.
  • Right pocket: Deodorant, microneedler, razor cartridges.
  • Left pocket: Pills (cold, sleep, antacid, ibuprofen), razor charger, whitening strips.
  • Inner mesh pockets: Facial sunscreen, toothpaste & floss, lens cleaner & microfiber.
  • Front mesh pocket: Contacts, nail clippers.

Electronics

  • Only own / use Thunderbolt-4 USB-C, and pack a number of USB adapters for different plugs. Fast charging and maximum compatibility.
  • Cellular router gives incredibly reliable internet almost everywhere. Much better than hotel WiFi.
  • Skross universal adapter works in 200 countries.
  • USB charger is 200 W and will charge everything way faster. It can rapidly charge laptop, phone, power brick, and cellular router at the same time.
  • Turtle coil neatens cables and is good for packing them.
  • Power brick is powerful enough to charge laptop, phone, etc. simultaneously.

Pack list:

  • Power brick, universal adapter, earphones, turtle coil with 3× USB-C cables, 1× Marq charger.
  • Bag 1: 3× USB-A, 2× USB-micro, USB-C to USB-A adapters; 2× USB-C extenders, USB-C cable, memory card.
  • Bag 2: USB charger.
  • Bag 3: Cellular router, mouse.
