Hiking in the Swiss Alps

Switzerland has over 65,000 km of marked hiking trails. If you did a new hike every weekend your entire life you would never finish them all. This is my informal introduction — covering how to find routes, read the weather, understand trail grades, and prepare properly for a safe day in the mountains.

Note: I am not a mountain guide or outdoor safety expert. This is an informal introduction, not professional advice. Mountain conditions can change rapidly — always check MeteoSwiss, the SAC Route Portal, and local tourism offices before setting out. This guide covers summer hiking only.
