Selection criteria

These 50 routes were chosen for their combination of scenic quality, cultural significance, variety of terrain, and accessibility by public transport. The list balances regions (Oberland, Wallis, Zentralschweiz, Ostschweiz, Graubünden, Ticino, Jura, Romandie) and difficulty levels (T1–T5) so that every hiker finds something.

This is a personal selection, not an official ranking.
