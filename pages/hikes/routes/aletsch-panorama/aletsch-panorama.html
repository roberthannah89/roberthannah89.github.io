Bettmerhorn 2857 m

SAC T4 · ~248 m gain · ~10.1 km one-way · ~5h round-trip

The tour is mountain hiking at its best. On Bettmergrat you can feel the cool breeze coming off Aletschgletscher, the centrepiece of the Unesco World Heritage Site whose border we walk on.

Bettmerhorn is part of a long ridge between Laxeralp and Grosser Aletschgletscher. The eastern summit has a big metal cross, while the highest point is crowned by a simple cairn. Bettmerhorn offers a rewarding traverse on the ridge between the Bettmergrat station and Eggishorn.
