Zindlenspitz 2097 m

SAC T5 · ~1432 m gain · ~11.3 km one-way · ~9h30 round-trip

A spectacular three-summit ridge traverse between the Wägital and Glarus. Starting from Wägitalersee, the route links Brünnelistock (2132 m), Rossälplispitz (2075 m) and Zindlenspitz (2097 m) — one of the great classics of the region. The crux is the exposed ridge to Brünnelistock (T5−); the rest ranges from T3 to T4 with fixed ropes and chains.

Routes (Three-summit ridge traverse from Wägitalersee)

Brünnelistock, Rossälplispitz and Zindlenspitz — ridge traverse T5

  • P. 931 via Hohfläsch-Hütte (1368 m) to Hohfläschenmatt (1687 m) — 2 h 15 min
  • Hohfläschenmatt to saddle P. 1989 — narrow, slightly exposed ledges without scrambling
  • Detour to Brünnelistock (2132 m) — exposed ridge, no belaying possibilities (T5−, crux)
  • Saddle P. 1989 to Rossälplispitz (2075 m) — fixed ropes on rocky summit slope (T3+)
  • Rossälplispitz to Zindlenspitz (2097 m) via P. 1979 — steep rocky outcrop with chain (T4−), easy scrambling before summit
  • Zindlenspitz descent to P. 931 — 2 h

Getting There

By car

Drive to Wägitalersee east shore. A few parking spaces at the Fläschloch cable car valley station, ~500 m past P. 932.

By public transport

Bus to Innerthal, Gemeindeplatz, then ~45 min on foot along the road to P. 932. Alternatives: rent a bike at Gasthaus Stausee (055 446 12 79), boat taxi via waegitalersee.ch (055 446 13 44), Alpentaxi, or Mobility from Siebnen-Wangen station.

Safety & Hazards

  • Exposure: The ridge from P. 1989 to Brünnelistock has exposed passages without belaying possibilities (T5−). Not suitable for those uncomfortable with exposure.
  • Wet conditions: SAC explicitly warns against undertaking this tour in wet conditions — exposed rock and ledges become very dangerous.
  • Fixed ropes/chains: Fixed ropes on Rossälplispitz and a chain after P. 1901 — check condition before relying on them.
  • Turn back if thunderstorms develop — lightning risk on the exposed ridge is extreme.
