Monte-Rosa-Hütte SAC 2883 m

SAC T4 · ~361 m gain · ~8.7 km one-way · ~4h30 round-trip

The second half of the normal approach to the Monte Rosa hut SAC is not a normal alpine hike, but a glacier tour: crampons, harness, rope and ice axe are essential. The approach is well marked white-blue-white, bars are placed on the glacier. With glacier shrinkage, the transition to the glacier and the route over it varies from year to year. In summer 2019 a new route, the "Panoramaweg Monte Rosa Hütte", was added, which is about quarter an hour shorter than the old way.

Trip Reports (hikr.org)

Key takeaways

  • The glacier crossing is the crux: the route over the Gornergletscher is marked with poles but crevasse danger is real, and conditions change year to year as the glacier retreats. Crampons and rope are strongly recommended.
  • The transition from glacier to polished rock below the hut and the moraine sections with loose scree are frequently cited hazards; several reports note the T4 rating understates the exposure.
  • Weather windows are critical at 2883 m — afternoon thunderstorms develop quickly and temperatures can drop below freezing even in summer. Early starts are advised.
