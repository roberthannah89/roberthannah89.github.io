Planurahütte SAC 2947 m

SAC T3+ · ~2164 m gain · ~15.0 km one-way · ~13h round-trip

Long hut approach that leads from the lush green Tierfehd to the high alpine glacier world around the Planura Hut SAC. With the alpine taxi to Hintersand, the walking time can be shortened by about one and a half hours.
