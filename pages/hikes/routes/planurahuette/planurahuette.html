Planurahütte SAC 2947 m

SAC T3+ · ~2164 m gain · ~15.0 km one-way · ~13h round-trip

Long hut approach that leads from the lush green Tierfehd to the high alpine glacier world around the Planura Hut SAC. With the alpine taxi to Hintersand, the walking time can be shortened by about one and a half hours.

Directions from to Tierfehd

Trip Reports (hikr.org)

Key takeaways

  • The long approach from Tierfehd (2100+ m gain) is physically very demanding and requires good fitness; many hikers use bikes or the alpine taxi to Hinter Sand to shorten the lower valley section.
  • The upper route above Ober Sand enters exposed rocky terrain with fixed chains around 2800 m; snow patches persist well into July.
  • The Planurahütte (renovated 2012, highest SAC hut in eastern Switzerland) offers a dramatic glacier setting and serves as the base for Tödi, Clariden, and glacier traverses.
