Cabane de Tracuit CAS 3256 m

SAC T4 · ~1581 m gain · ~7.9 km one-way · ~8h30 round-trip

Long, but charming approach. The less difficult, even longer approach via the Roc de la Vache is a nice alternative. Or if you prefer a challenge and some exposure, try the variant over the Pas du Chasseur (for details see approach for the Cabane d 'Arpitettaz).
