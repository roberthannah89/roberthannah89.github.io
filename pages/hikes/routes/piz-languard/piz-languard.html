Piz Languard 3263 m

SAC T4- · ~549 m gain · ~8.3 km one-way · ~5h round-trip

The easy accessibility of the summit and the cabin high up cannot hide the fact that the trail is steep, stony and at times exposed. Thanks to Georgys Hütte it is uncomplicated to experience sunrise and sunset surrounded by majestic mountains. Observing ibex is almost guaranteed.

Piz Languard, a popular viewpoint, is well accessible on a path from Pontresina. Until high up, the southern and western faces consist of pastures, while the summit structure does not begin before an altitude of ca. 2900 m. Its eastern face falls steeply down to Val Prüna. Three ridges merge on the summit. The eastern ridge is the wildest. Piz Languard is most impressive when viewed from the east. W. Rey in 1846
