Spannorthuette Sac 1960 m

SAC T3 · ~888 m gain · ~6.3 km one-way · ~5h30 round-trip

The ascent from Engelberg to the Spannorthütte begins comfortably along the Engelberger Aa (river), but quickly steepens under the walls of the Schlossberg. Although the approach is designated as a white-red-white hiking trail, it has a few exposed passages and even a short climbing section. The classification as T3 therefore seems justified.
