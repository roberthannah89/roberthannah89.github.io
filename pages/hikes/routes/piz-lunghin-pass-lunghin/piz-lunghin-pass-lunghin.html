Piz Lunghin Pass Lunghin 2780 m

SAC T3 · ~985 m gain · ~14.6 km one-way · ~9h round-trip

The local mountain above Maloja is a marvellous, easily accessible viewpoint, which is entirely marked and thus often visited. The idyllic Lägh dal Lunghin, the three-way European watershed on Pass Lunghin and the historic trail across Septimerpass make the hike an overall experience of nature, culture and history.

Piz Grevasalvas: ascend to Pass Lunghin as described, then pathless turn northward to reach an escarpment with brittle rock. Bypass it on the right (east) following a faint path. Then again on the ridge, ascend until P. 2793. North-eastward traverse a depression towards P. 2814. Pass it on the right and ascend to the summit (some cairns). On the upper part the descent is almost pathless, but marked white-red-white. Descend to Lej Nair, Plaun Grand, Grevasalvas and Plaun da Lej. Maloja – Piz Grevasalvas 3¾ h, T4+, Piz Grevasalvas – Plaun da Lej 2½ h, T3.

Terrain & safety

The closer the summit, the stonier the trails. Nevertheless, the summit is easy to reach. Until Pass Lunghin the trail is marked white-red-white, the ascent from the col to the summit white-blue-white.
