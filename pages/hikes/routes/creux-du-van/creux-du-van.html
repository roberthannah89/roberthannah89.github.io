Le Soliat (Creux du Van) 1464 m

SAC T4 · ~736 m gain · ~9.1 km one-way · ~6h round-trip

The Dos d'Âne (back of a donkey) is likely the most attractive itinerary to the popular Creux du Van: an entertaining hike with scrambling passages.

Le Soliat is a popular hiking target on the rim of Creux du Van. Roads, farms and several inns characterise the wide high plain between Lac de Neuchâtel and Val de Travers. On the highest point there is a summit cross and a panoramic display protected against the grazing cattle by a wooden fence. In the area there is also an ibex colony.
