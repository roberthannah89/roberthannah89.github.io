Piz d'Agnel 3204 m

SAC T4 · ~1241 m gain · ~5.3 km one-way · ~6h30 round-trip

Alp Flix with its overwhelming diversity of plant life is a hiking destination in its own right. For those who not only want to enjoy the beauty of the high moor landscape, but wish to climb an attractive mountain, Piz d’Agnel is their first choice. The ascent is demanding, but exciting and rewarded by a splendid panorama.

This summit with an equally splendid view as Piz d’Agnel can be added with reasonable effort, starting from Fuorcla da Flix. T3, 1½ h, 250 metres of vertical height.
