Risetestock 1758 m

Gfellen → Risetestock → Blaue Tosse — SAC T4 · ~820 m gain · ~4 h traverse

The Risetestock (1758 m) is a western outcrop of the Pilatus range, rising above the small hamlet of Gfellen in the Entlebuch. The SAC-rated T4 traverse from Gfellen to Blaue Tosse crosses exposed terrain opposite the Schimberig, with 820 m of ascent through steep alpine ground.
