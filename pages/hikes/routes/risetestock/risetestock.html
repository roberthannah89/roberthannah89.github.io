Risetestock 1758 m

Gfellen → Risetestock → Blaue Tosse — SAC T4 · ~820 m gain · ~4 h traverse

The Risetestock (1758 m) is a western outcrop of the Pilatus range, rising above the small hamlet of Gfellen in the Entlebuch. The SAC-rated T4 traverse from Gfellen to Blaue Tosse crosses exposed terrain opposite the Schimberig, with 820 m of ascent through steep alpine ground.

Trip Reports (hikr.org)

Key takeaways

  • Exposed ridge sections with iron rungs and fixed ropes; sure-footedness and a head for heights essential. Loose rung reported at rock-band transition.
  • Partly pathless terrain between Risetestock and Blaue Tosse — cairns and faint trail traces above ~1500 m; good route-finding skills needed.
  • Isolated summit with panoramic views across the Pilatus chain and Entlebuch; alpine-rose fields just below the summit in early summer.

Safety & Hazards

  • T4 terrain: Exposed sections require sure-footedness and a head for heights. Hands may be needed in places.
  • Wet conditions: Steep grass and rock become dangerous when wet — postpone if rain is forecast.
  • Turn back if thunderstorms develop — lightning risk above treeline is extreme.
