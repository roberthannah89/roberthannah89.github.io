Swiss Weather Layers

MeteoSwiss live stations + RainViewer radar + climate normals

Live Station Data

Real-time measurements from ~160 MeteoSwiss automatic weather stations, updated every 10 minutes. Fetched as GeoJSON and rendered as circle markers.
Temperature
Air temperature at 2m above ground. Color scale: blue (<0°C) → green (10°C) → red (30°C+).
GeoJSON
Precipitation
10-minute precipitation sum in mm. Sized by intensity — larger circles = more rain.
GeoJSON
Wind Gusts
Maximum 1-second gust in last 10 minutes (km/h). Color scale: green (calm) → orange → red (storm).
GeoJSON
Humidity
Relative humidity (%). Low values indicate dry air, high values suggest fog or precipitation risk.
GeoJSON
Fresh Snow (1 day)
New snowfall in last 24 hours (cm). Only populated at high-altitude stations.
GeoJSON
MeteoSwiss Webcams
35 official MeteoSwiss weather cameras. Click markers for links to live feeds.
GeoJSON

Radar

Global precipitation radar from RainViewer. Free API, no auth. Shows past observations + short-term nowcast. Use the slider to scrub through time.
Rain Radar
Precipitation radar with time slider. Green = light, yellow = moderate, red = heavy, purple = extreme.
Radar tiles

Climate Normals (WMTS)

30-year average maps (1991–2020). These are static reference layers — not live data. Useful for understanding typical conditions in an area.
