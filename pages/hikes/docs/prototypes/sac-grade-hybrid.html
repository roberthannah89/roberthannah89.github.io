Zindlenspitz — hybrid T-grade segmentation (OSM sac_scale + swissTLM3D Wanderwege)

Prototype only. Each GPX point first tries to snap to an OSM way tagged sac_scale=* within 25–50 m (precise T1–T6). If that fails, it snaps to a swissTLM3D Wanderwege trail within 15–30 m and inherits a coarse bucket (Wanderweg→T1, Bergwanderweg→T2/T3, Alpinwanderweg→T4+). Points with no match within either tolerance are unknown. Smoothed with a 7-point majority filter. Coarse buckets are shown in muted colors with a small swissTLM3D tag so it's clear they're less precise than the OSM-derived grades.
