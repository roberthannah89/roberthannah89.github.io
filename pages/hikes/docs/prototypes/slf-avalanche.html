European Avalanche Danger Scale

Reference
1

Low

Snowpack generally well bonded. Triggering possible only with high additional loads on very few extreme steep slopes. Only small natural avalanches possible.

2

Moderate

Snowpack only moderately bonded on some steep slopes. Triggering possible with high additional loads, particularly on indicated steep slopes. Large natural avalanches unlikely.

3

Considerable

Snowpack moderately to poorly bonded on many steep slopes. Triggering possible even with low additional loads. In certain situations some medium, and occasionally large, natural avalanches possible.

4

High

Snowpack poorly bonded on most steep slopes. Triggering likely even with low additional loads on many steep slopes. In some situations frequent medium and occasionally large natural avalanches expected.

5

Very High

Snowpack generally poorly bonded and largely unstable. Many large and often very large natural avalanches expected, even in moderately steep terrain. Avoid all alpine travel.
