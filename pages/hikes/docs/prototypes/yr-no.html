About this forecast

Model: MetCoOp HARMONIE on a 2.5 km grid, run jointly by the Norwegian and Swedish met offices. Beyond ~66 hours the forecast blends in ECMWF IFS global model output. MetCoOp is widely regarded as one of the strongest mesoscale models for mountainous terrain — including the Alps — thanks to its high resolution and assimilation of dense observation networks.

Locationforecast
10-day time series. Hourly resolution for the first 48 hours, 6-hourly thereafter. Returns temperature, wind, precipitation, cloud cover, humidity, pressure and a Yr weather symbol code.
Nowcast
High-resolution precipitation nowcast for the next 90 minutes in 5-minute steps. Based on radar extrapolation. Coverage is limited to Nordic countries — in Switzerland you may get an empty time series.
Sunrise 3.0
Sunrise, sunset, solar noon and twilight times for any location and date. Useful for planning early starts and turn-around times on long alpine days.
