About the 7Timer! forecast

Cloud cover Scale 1–9. 1 = 0–6% clear sky, 9 = 94–100% overcast. Each step is roughly 12% cloud.
Seeing Scale 1–8 in arcseconds. 1 = <0.5″ (excellent), 8 = >2.5″ (very poor). Affects star sharpness through a telescope.
Transparency Scale 1–8 in mag/airmass. 1 = <0.3 (very clear), 8 = >1.0 (very hazy). Affects faint star visibility.
Lifted index Atmospheric instability. Negative values mean rising air parcels stay warmer than surroundings — the engine of thunderstorms. LI < -2 = unstable, < -6 = severe.
Data source Forecast derived from NOAA GFS model, post-processed by 7Timer! Free, no API key, updated 4×/day.
Resolution 3-hour timesteps for 8 days ahead. Spatial resolution roughly 0.25° (~28 km). Best for general planning, not micro-conditions.
