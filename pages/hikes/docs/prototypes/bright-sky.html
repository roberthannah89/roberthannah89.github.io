About Bright Sky & DWD

1 Bright Sky is a free, open-source JSON API wrapper around the DWD OpenData service published by Germany's national weather service (Deutscher Wetterdienst).
2 Why use it for Swiss hikes? The DWD's ICON-EU (7 km) and ICON-D2 (2.2 km) models have excellent coverage of the northern Alps and are particularly strong on precipitation. They complement MeteoSwiss for cross-border areas, the Bodensee, eastern Switzerland and the Jura.
3 No API key, CORS-enabled. All requests can be made directly from the browser. Data is licensed under CC-BY-4.0 — attribute "Deutscher Wetterdienst" when republishing.
4 What you get: current weather (last observation), hourly forecast up to 10 days ahead, nearest source station metadata, and 5-minute precipitation radar covering Germany and surrounding regions.
Times are converted to CET/CEST in this prototype (Bright Sky returns UTC). For Swiss-only forecasts, prefer MeteoSwiss. For high-resolution coverage close to the German border (Alpstein, Schaffhausen, Bodensee, northern Jura), Bright Sky frequently has finer detail than the standard global models.
