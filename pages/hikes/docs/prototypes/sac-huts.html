How to book an SAC hut

1 Create an SAC account. Register at sac-cas.ch. SAC members receive a discount on overnight stays (roughly half price).
2 Find and reserve online. Use the SAC Route Portal to find a hut page, then click the reservation link. Most wardened huts use the Alps Online booking system.
3 Pack a sleeping bag liner. Huts provide blankets and pillows but no sheets. Bring a lightweight liner (silk or cotton). Half-board (dinner + breakfast) is standard at wardened huts.
4 Know the hut etiquette. Arrive before dinner (usually 18:00–19:00), switch to hut shoes at the entrance, respect quiet hours from 22:00, and settle your bill before departure.
Self-service huts are unstaffed. You need your own food, a sleeping bag, and cash to leave the overnight fee in the honesty box. Some have a winter room that stays open year-round. Check the SAC hut page for current conditions before heading out. SAC membership card or Swiss Alpine Club reciprocal membership is required for the reduced rate.

SAC membership benefits

♦ Reduced hut rates. Members pay approximately CHF 30–40 per night; non-members pay CHF 60–80. The savings add up quickly over a season.
♦ Reciprocal rights. SAC membership is recognised by alpine clubs across Europe (DAV, OeAV, CAI, CAF, etc.) for reduced rates at their huts.
♦ Rescue insurance. Membership includes CHF 25,000 of rescue cost coverage in Switzerland through Rega and ground-based rescue services.
Join online at sac-cas.ch — Become a Member. You join through a local SAC section (e.g. SAC Zurich, SAC Bern). Annual fee is around CHF 120–150 for individuals depending on the section.
