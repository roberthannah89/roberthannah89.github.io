Apps & resources

Apps, websites, maps, and data sources for hiking in Switzerland.

Apps

AppWhat it does
SwissTopo Official 1:25,000 topographic maps with offline download. Import GPX tracks, overlay hiking trails and SAC huts. Essential for T4+ navigation. See Planning.
Komoot Custom route planning with elevation profiles, per-segment SAC grades, user photos, turn-by-turn navigation, and GPX export. See Planning.
SchweizMobil Official national trail network with route numbers, public transit integration, and GPS tracking.
PeakFinder AR panorama tool — point your phone at any summit and it labels every peak in view. Great for orientation on ridgelines.
Rega Free mountain rescue app. Transmits GPS coordinates on emergency call, works via SMS fallback in poor coverage. Always install before heading out. See Gear & safety.
MeteoSwiss Swiss national weather service — high-resolution alpine forecast model, radar, and satellite. The go-to forecast. See Weather.
SBB Mobile Swiss Federal Railways timetable and tickets. Covers trains, PostBus, and many gondolas. See Planning.

Websites & portals

ResourceWhat it is
SAC Route Portal The definitive database of Swiss alpine routes — 6,000+ curated routes with difficulty ratings, GPX files, and hut booking. See Planning for a full walkthrough.
map.wanderland.ch Official interactive map of the entire Swiss trail network. Shows all national, regional, and local routes with GPS tracking.
SchweizMobil / Wanderland Route browser for all signposted hiking routes. GPX downloads, public transport links, stage planning. See Trail system.
MySwitzerland Switzerland Tourism's hiking portal — curated selections by theme, region, and season.
AllTrails User-contributed trip reports, photos, and trail conditions.
Suisse Rando The national hiking federation — trail standards, advocacy, cantonal organisation directory.

Maps & data

ResourceWhat it is
swisstopo hiking maps Official 1:25,000 and 1:50,000 topographic maps with trail overlays. Available as paper maps and in the SwissTopo app.
opendata.swiss — SchweizMobil Open data: the full SchweizMobil route dataset as GeoJSON. Free to download and use.
opendata.swiss — swissTLM3D trails Open data: the complete Swiss trail network geometry from swisstopo. GeoPackage format.
