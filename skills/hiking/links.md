## Tool notes

### Swiss Alpine Club (SAC)

Comprehensive hub for outdoor activities. Use SAC first for route quality, hut info,
access notes, and condition warnings.

### SAC Route Portal

6,000+ curated routes and 150+ huts. Filter by activity (mountain hiking, alpine hiking,
ski touring, snowshoeing, climbing). Each route lists duration, difficulty, elevation
gain/loss, hazard warnings, and protected-area restrictions.

### SAC Interactive Map

Built on SwissTopo. Download GPX for offline GPS use. Topographic overlays with altitude
profiles. Use layers to inspect trail class, steepness, and nearby huts.

**Map legend:**

- **Red trails**: T1-T3 (mountain hiking)
- **Blue trails**: T4-T6 (alpine hiking)
- **Red house icons**: alpine huts
- **Green peaks**: hiking peaks
- **Blue peaks**: ski-tour peaks
- **Purple peaks**: alpine peaks
- **Multicolor peaks**: multiple route options

### Komoot

Build your own routes (web + mobile, links to Strava). Detailed steepness and SAC trail
rating along the entire route. User photos for reference. Download GPX and navigate from
your phone.

### SwissTopo App

Trail and routing info on the go. Download maps for offline use — cell reception is
unreliable in the mountains.

### MeteoSwiss

Official Swiss national weather service. High-resolution forecasts, real-time radar,
satellite imagery. Most accurate forecast for Swiss terrain.

### Meteoblue

Useful secondary model and hourly detail. Webcams ground-truth conditions (cloud, fog,
snow cover).
